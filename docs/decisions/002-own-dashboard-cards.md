# ADR-002: A redesigned view draws with our own Lovelace cards

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-10-17). Kodu, the first view drawn with the cards, went back to stock cards on 2026-10-18: the operator rejected the A2 direction, not the cards. The next design round decides which view uses them.
- **Related:** [ADR-001](001-own-3d-engine.md), [estate ADR-007](https://github.com/khelias/khe-architecture/blob/main/decisions/007-automated-writers.md)

## Context

The dashboard is generated by `scripts/dashboard_config.py` from HA's stock
cards: tile, markdown, heading, statistics-graph and apexcharts-card. Kodu,
the front view, was a stack of those: badges for people, alarms and faults,
a markdown price summary, a price chart, three usage tiles, two 7-day bar
charts and one section per system with its heading as the way into that
system's view. It worked and read as a settings page.

On 2026-10-17 a design round on a
[canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/7qtyRL9XkAa43peBBipWqH) compared three
directions on the demo house's made-up data, and the operator picked A2, a
calm view pushed towards Apple Home:

- one sentence that says whether everything is fine, with summary chips
  under it;
- the electricity price as one number over 48 hourly bars;
- the devices as tiles that are lit when on, and a segmented control for
  the ventilation mode;
- the same layout in dark colours at night.

Stock cards draw none of the first three: a tile has no lit/unlit card
background, a markdown card cannot compose a sentence from a list of
entities without a template per house, and no stock card draws the bars.
The design comes first; where a stock card cannot draw it, the repo writes
its own card, as it did for the 3D view (ADR-001).

The cards are content of the House room (`khe.ee/ha`, where the demo is
published) in the [KHE Family](https://claude.ai/artifact/FBee1HXuEELvs3MiAQRVfA)
design system. Its rule is that room content that is an application keeps
its own visual language and the family layer covers the shell, as the Games
room already does.

## Decision

1. **Kodu is drawn with our own vanilla-JS Lovelace cards inside HA's
   sections view.** One ES module, `cards/khe-cards.js`, defines
   `khe-summary-card`, `khe-price-card` and `khe-tile-card`, in the house3d
   card's pattern: an `HTMLElement` with a shadow root around `<ha-card>`,
   pure helpers exported for the Node tests, no build step and no CDN
   (ADR-001), and a `KHE_CARDS_VERSION` constant matched by `?v=` on the
   Lovelace resource. Deploy copies `cards/` to `www/khe-cards/`.
2. **Colours come from `hass.themes.darkMode` inside the cards**, with no
   HA theme file. The palette is the canvas's, light and dark, and a card
   redraws when the flag changes.
3. **The status sentence is composed in the card** from the entities the
   generator passes (faults with their bad state, alarm panels, people and
   the price level), not in a template sensor. `FAULTS`, `ALARMS` and the
   people list stay single-sourced in `dashboard_config.py`.
4. **Actions go through HA.** A card fires `hass-action` with
   `{config: {entity, tap_action}, action: 'tap'}`, bubbling and composed,
   and HA's `handleAction` runs the confirmation, more-info, navigate,
   toggle or perform-action. Confirmed in frontend 20260826.7 (HA 2026.9.4):
   `src/state/action-mixin.ts:19` and
   `src/panels/lovelace/common/handle-action.ts:28`.

## Alternatives considered

- **Mushroom or Bubble Card** (HACS). A dependency the house would have to
  keep current, and still unable to draw A2: neither has the sentence, the
  hourly bars or a lit tile background without card-mod.
- **card-mod on stock tiles.** CSS on HA's internal shadow DOM, which
  changes between HA releases without notice; every bump would be a visual
  regression hunt.
- **One panel card for the whole view.** Draws anything, but loses the
  sections layout (columns on a wide screen, reflow on a phone), the
  per-card `visibility` conditions and the generator's per-section
  structure that the other views share.

## Consequences

- Every redesigned view adds its elements to the one module; the other
  views stay on stock cards until each gets its own design round.
- HA frontend bumps can change how `hass-action` is handled. The HA bump
  check is in the dashboard cards section of `docs/operational-notes.md`:
  after each bump, tap a Kodu tile and a mode button and see HA act.
- The demo mounts each new element on its simulated hass, so a card that
  breaks shows on `khe.ee/ha` before it reaches the house.
- The cards follow KHE Family's room-world rule: they take the family's
  `paper`, `ink` and `ink-2` and draw the price and summary between rules
  without a card box, and keep their own state colours and tiles. Tiles
  stay cards because they are pressed.
- An HA theme or a view background is a separate decision: it needs
  `frontend:` YAML, a restart and choosing the theme on each device.
